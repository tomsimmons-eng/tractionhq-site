Google Business Profile and AI search visibility

When someone nearby asks Google, Apple Maps, or ChatGPT for a business like yours, your listings decide whether you show up. We claim them, fix them, and keep them consistent.

Book a free consultation

The problem we solve

Listings get created by customers, data brokers, or a past employee, and then drift: old hours, wrong categories, a disconnected phone number, no photos. Inconsistent details make search engines and AI assistants less confident about recommending you.

What we do

  • Claim, verify, and fully complete your Google Business Profile: categories, services, service area, hours, photos, and description.
  • Set up Bing Places for Business and Apple Business Connect, which feed Bing, Copilot, Apple Maps, and Siri.
  • Claim your business in ChatGPT's Business Hub where your business type is eligible, so the details ChatGPT shows come from you.
  • Make your name, contact details, and service area consistent across major directories.
  • Set up a simple, honest review request process for happy customers.
  • Post updates and answer common questions so your profiles stay active.

When it makes sense

This is often the fastest visible win for a local business. Listings are free to claim, and fixing them usually takes days, not months.

How it fits a weekly engagement

This work is delivered by our forward deployed engineers as part of a simple weekly engagement with no contract. See pricing for details, or read our FAQ.

Frequently asked questions

Can you get my business listed in ChatGPT?

OpenAI's Business Hub lets eligible businesses claim and manage how they appear in ChatGPT. Eligibility depends on business type and location. ChatGPT also draws on web search results, so your website, Bing presence, and directory listings still matter. We handle all of it.

Do I need a storefront for a Google Business Profile?

No. Service-area businesses that visit customers can hide their address and list the areas they serve instead.

Will you write fake reviews?

Never. We help you ask real customers for reviews in ways that follow each platform's rules.
