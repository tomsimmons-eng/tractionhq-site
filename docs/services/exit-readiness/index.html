Exit and sale readiness

Whether you plan to sell in two years or just want the business to run without you, the work is the same: document how it runs, clean up the numbers, and reduce dependence on the owner.

Book a free consultation

The problem we solve

A business that only runs because the owner is there is hard to sell and stressful to own. Buyers and lenders look for documented processes, reliable reporting, and systems that will keep working after the handoff.

What we do

  • Document core processes so the business can run without the owner in every decision.
  • Clean up and standardize KPIs and reporting so performance is easy to show.
  • Modernize systems and remove fragile workarounds, spreadsheets, and single points of failure.
  • Organize the information a buyer typically asks for during due diligence.
  • Shift routine work from the owner to the team, supported by tools and training.
  • Coordinate with your accountant, attorney, or business broker, without replacing them.

When it makes sense

We focus on operations and technology. Valuation, legal, and tax advice should come from licensed professionals, and we're happy to work alongside yours.

How it fits a weekly engagement

This work is delivered by our forward deployed engineers as part of a simple weekly engagement with no contract. See pricing for details, or read our FAQ.

Frequently asked questions

How early should I start preparing to sell?

Ideally a year or more ahead, so improvements show up in your results. But even a few months of work on documentation and reporting can make a sale smoother.

Do you value businesses or broker sales?

No. We prepare the operations and systems. Valuation and the sale itself belong with a broker, accountant, and attorney.

What if I'm not selling?

The same work makes a business more efficient and less stressful to run day to day.
