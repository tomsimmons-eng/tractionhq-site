AI is everywhere. Making it useful is the hard part.

Most small business owners know AI and modern software could save them time and money. Few have the hours to figure out which tools matter, how to connect them, or how to get employees to use them.

So it stalls. A few people try ChatGPT on their own. The website goes stale. Leads come in through five channels and nobody tracks them. The business runs on the owner's memory instead of a process.

Large companies solve this with in-house technology teams. Small businesses deserve the same advantage at a price that makes sense. That's what we do.

Forward deployed engineers, not slide decks

A forward deployed engineer works inside your business instead of from a distance. They don't hand you a report and leave. They learn how work actually gets done, then build, connect, and configure the tools that fix the bottlenecks they find.

  1. Discover and map. We learn how your business runs and where it loses time, money, or leads.
  2. Plan and measure. We agree on priorities and 3 to 5 KPIs that show progress.
  3. Build and integrate. We set up the AI, software, and integrations your plan calls for.
  4. Train and adopt. We work alongside your team until the new tools are part of the routine.
  5. Review and improve. We check the numbers with you and adjust.

Common questions

What is a forward deployed engineer?

A technologist who works inside your business instead of from a distance. They learn how work actually gets done, then build, connect, and configure the tools that fix the bottlenecks, and stay until your team is using them.

How much does it cost?

Pricing is weekly. Tier 1, for businesses under $1M in revenue with 1 to 10 employees, is $1,250 per week. Businesses with $1M to $3M in revenue and up to 15 employees get a custom quote. The cost listed does not include subscriptions, token usage, and other expenses, which are kept to a minimum for the client's benefit.

Do I have to sign a contract?

No. There are no contracts. You pay as you go, week by week, and can stop anytime.

What areas do you serve?

Charlotte and the surrounding area, including Waxhaw, Matthews, Ballantyne, Mint Hill, Huntersville, Cornelius, Davidson, Concord, Indian Trail, Monroe, and Fort Mill and Rock Hill in South Carolina. Much of the work can also be done remotely.
