FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about what we do, what it costs, and how we work.

Book a free consultation

What does Traction Consulting do?

We're an AI-focused business and technology consultancy for small businesses in the Charlotte area. Our forward deployed engineers work inside your business to map how it runs, set up and connect the right tools (AI, websites, custom software, integrations), train your team, and track results with KPIs.

What is a forward deployed engineer?

A technologist who works inside your business instead of from a distance. They learn how work actually gets done, then build, connect, and configure the tools that fix the bottlenecks, and stay until your team is using them.

How much does it cost?

Pricing is weekly. Tier 1, for businesses under $1M in revenue with 1 to 10 employees, is $1,250 per week. Businesses with $1M to $3M in revenue and up to 15 employees get a custom quote. The cost listed does not include subscriptions, token usage, and other expenses, which are kept to a minimum for the client's benefit.

Do I have to sign a contract?

No. There are no contracts. You pay as you go, week by week, and can stop anytime.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

Owner-led small businesses, generally under $3M in revenue with up to 15 employees, in the Charlotte metro. Professional services, trades, retail, and other local businesses are all a good fit.

What areas do you serve?

Charlotte and the surrounding area, including Waxhaw, Matthews, Ballantyne, Mint Hill, Huntersville, Cornelius, Davidson, Concord, Indian Trail, Monroe, and Fort Mill and Rock Hill in South Carolina. Much of the work can also be done remotely.

Is the first consultation really free?

Yes. The first conversation is free and there's no obligation. We'll learn how your business runs and tell you honestly whether we can help.

How many clients do you take on?

We keep a limited client roster so the engineer who learns your business is the one building for it, and your work doesn't wait in a long queue.

Will AI replace my employees?

Our goal is to make your existing team more productive by taking repetitive work off their plates, so they can spend more time on customers and higher-value work.

Is my business data safe?

We work only inside the access you grant, prefer accounts you own, keep sensitive data out of public AI tools unless you approve it in writing, and remove our access when an engagement ends. Where data is sensitive, a privately hosted LLM can keep it on infrastructure you control.

Who owns the work you create?

You do. Websites, code, documentation, and accounts are set up in your name so you're never locked in.
