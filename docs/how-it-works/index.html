How it works: forward deployed engineers

We don't sell software and walk away, and we don't hand over a slide deck. We embed with your team, build what's needed, and stay until it works.

Book a free consultation

What is a forward deployed engineer?

The term comes from technology companies that send engineers to work directly inside a customer's organization instead of supporting them from headquarters. A forward deployed engineer learns the customer's real problems up close and builds solutions on the spot.

We bring that model to small businesses. Your engineer sits with your team (in person or online), learns how work actually gets done, and then builds, connects, and configures the tools that fix the bottlenecks they find. Behind them, AI agents handle research, first drafts, and routine build work, which is how we keep the price within reach of a small business. A person reviews and approves everything before it reaches you.

How an engagement runs

  1. Free consultation. A no-obligation conversation about your business and what's slowing it down. We'll tell you honestly if we're not the right fit.
  2. Foundation (first two weeks). Discovery and process mapping, a KPI baseline, an audit of the tools and subscriptions you already pay for, and quick wins such as fixing your Google Business Profile. You get a written roadmap.
  3. Build and integrate. One main workstream at a time from the roadmap: an automation, an integration, website pages, an AI assistant, or a training program.
  4. Train and adopt. We work with your employees until the new tools become part of their routine.
  5. Measure and adjust. We review your KPIs with you regularly and re-prioritize based on what the numbers say.

What a typical week looks like

What we always keep human

  • A named person leads your engagement and is your point of contact.
  • Client meetings, on-site work, and sensitive conversations are handled by people.
  • Nothing goes live in your business without a person reviewing it and your approval.
  • You control access to your systems and data, and we remove ours when we're done.

Frequently asked questions

What is a forward deployed engineer?

A technologist who works inside your business instead of from a distance. They learn how work actually gets done, then build, connect, and configure the tools that fix the bottlenecks, and stay until your team is using them.

How much time does my team need to give?

Usually a short weekly check-in plus time for training sessions and occasional shadowing. We work around your schedule and do most of the build work without pulling people off their jobs.

Do you work on-site?

When it helps. Shadowing, training, and kickoffs often happen in person in the Charlotte area. Building and configuration are usually done remotely.
